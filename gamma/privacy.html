Privacy Policy

Gamma: Video Editor · Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what information the Gamma: Video Editor app ("Gamma", "the app") for Android and iOS handles, and why. Gamma is developed by Nader Sayed ("we", "us"). If you have questions, contact us at llevelupdev@gmail.com.

Summary

  • Your videos, photos and projects stay on your device. We do not upload them to our servers.
  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email or phone number.
  • The free version shows ads. Our advertising partners may collect device information, such as your advertising ID, to show and measure ads.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple and Google. We never see your payment details.

1. Information processed on your device

To edit videos, Gamma needs access to some features of your device. Everything below is processed locally on your device and is not sent to us.

  • Photos and videos: to import media you choose and to save your edited videos to your gallery. On recent Android and iOS versions you pick files through the system picker, so the app only sees what you select.
  • Camera and microphone: only when you choose to record a new video inside the app.
  • Music and audio files: only when you choose to add audio to a project.
  • App settings: preferences such as theme and language are stored on your device.

Gamma does not collect your location. You can turn any permission off at any time in your device settings.

2. Advertising

The free version of Gamma shows ads (banner, full-screen and rewarded ads) provided by third-party advertising networks and their partners. These may include Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), Unity Ads, Yandex Ads and other networks that bid to show an ad in the app. When an ad is requested, these partners may automatically collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language, app version and network type;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address (country or city level);
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped.

They use this information to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, where you have agreed, show ads that match your interests. Each partner handles this information under its own privacy policy:

  • Unity / ironSource privacy policy
  • Yandex privacy policy

Your choices about ads

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or a region with similar laws, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice later from the app.
  • iOS: the app asks for permission before your IDFA is used for tracking (App Tracking Transparency). You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Remove ads: you can buy "Remove Ads" in the app. After that, no ads are requested.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We receive only a confirmation that a purchase is valid, so the app can unlock the feature. We do not receive your card number, billing address or other payment details. See the Apple privacy policy and the Google privacy policy.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share any information with third parties except the advertising and store partners described above. When you share a video using the share button, it goes to the app you choose (for example, WhatsApp or Instagram), under that app's own policy.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your projects and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting a project removes it from the app, and uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device. Information collected by advertising partners is kept according to their policies. To ask a partner to delete data linked to your advertising ID, reset your advertising ID (see above) or contact that partner.

6. Children

Gamma is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use for advertising. Because we do not store personal information on our own servers, most requests are handled through the advertising partners above or your device settings. You can always contact us at llevelupdev@gmail.com and we will help.

8. Security

Your media never leaves your device unless you share it yourself. Communication with advertising and store partners uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add a new feature or advertising partner. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
